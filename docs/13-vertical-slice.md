# 13 — Vertical Slice Plan

## 1. Purpose

Prove that SAFETRACE is a distinct game, not a description of one. The slice is
"one afternoon in Bellhaven": roughly 20–25 minutes, containing the complete
emotional arc in miniature.

## 3. Beat sheet

1. **Advertisement** (~75 s). Bellhaven from above, sunlit, in motion. Kids
   skate. A family walks. A drone passes and nobody looks up. Product lines
   appear. The wordmark resolves. `SAFETRACE™`.
2. **The same street, playable.** The exact geometry from the ad's final shot.
   Devon is on the kerb. Free skating; the town teaches itself.
3. **Errand.** Devon wants to get to the Channel. This is a movement tutorial
   disguised as a conversation, and it establishes the friendship in about six
   lines.
4. **The Channel.** Speed, flow, and the first place with no coverage — which
   the player will not consciously notice yet.
5. **The incident.** A burglary is reported in Northgate. Phones buzz. It is
   background noise. Devon makes a joke about it.
6. **The match.** `FACIAL MATCH CONFIRMED — 98.7% — ARAYA, DEVON M.` Devon is
   standing right there.
7. **The crack.** A drone's shadow crosses them. As its light comes on, the
   world flickers into MACHINE for 1.8 seconds — cones, brackets, edges, IDs —
   and snaps back. Neither character comments on it.
8. **Devon is stopped.** Not arrested. Just... stopped, and asked to wait, very
   politely, while the system checks. The player can do nothing and must watch.
9. **The player goes looking.** Objective: reach `CM-207` in Northgate. Requires
   the full toolkit — a covered route, one slingshot disruption to open a seam,
   one LOOP on a camera, one drone evaded under the parking decks.
10. **The records.** QUERY/TRACE chain reveals the pipeline that produced 98.7%.
    Nothing is broken. Nothing was malicious.
11. **VISION unlocks properly.** Not as a gift; as a comprehension. The peel
    plays at full length for the first time and the player can hold it.
12. **The reprise.** The advertisement replays, identical, with VISION active.
    Every frame is now annotated. The happy kid skating has a bracket and a
    score. The final line lands differently: *Someone is always looking out.*

## 4. Success criteria

The slice ships when all of these are true:

- A player who has never seen the game can skate a clean line through Maple
  Court within four minutes without instruction.
- The slingshot-into-camera-without-stopping move happens accidentally, and
  then deliberately.
- At least one playtester says something like "how did it know I was going
  there?" unprompted.
- At least one playtester takes a route because they remember the coverage, not
  because a marker told them to.
- The peel makes people stop talking.
- The advertisement reprise gets a reaction.

## 5. Explicitly out of slice scope

Grinds and manuals; Northgate as a full district; Priya Venn as an on-screen
character; the Relay 12 infiltration; night; multiple save profiles; controller
rumble tuning; localisation beyond string extraction.
